Hi! I am a Bioinformatician and Chemical Engineer, with a passion for public health. Working at the US National Institute of Health, I have supported research groups in sequencing data analysis for various clinical and research projects focused on diseases of the immune system (such as Hyper Eosinophilic Syndrome, Spondyloarthritis, Type 1 Interferonopathy, etc.) I am trained in immunology, and human and cancer genomics. I specialize in Single Cell and Bulk RNA sequencing data analysis, along with T-Cell Repertoire analysis. A more comprehensive list of my skills and certifications is [linked here](https://anshudeewan.github.io/skills/){:target="_blank"}. 

I started my career in Bioinformatics during my doctoral research at the University of Illinois, where I focused on the cellular mechanisms of yeasts and bacteria using various genomics and bioinformatics data analyses. In the 8+ years of my genomics career, I have analyzed next generation sequencing data for more than 30 projects, involving many organisms (bacterial, yeasts, mouse, monkey and human genomes). I have led data analysis projects, both independently and in interdisciplinary groups, to generate meaningful insights from sequencing data. My publication record is [linked here](https://anshudeewan.github.io/publications/){:target="_blank"}. 

I have also previously been a part of [Unilever Future Leaders Programme](https://careers.unilever.com/unilever-future-leaders-programme){:target="_blank"} and worked as a Product Manager with [Unilever Research](https://www.hul.co.in/our-company/rd-innovation/our-rd-locations/mumbai-india/){:target="_blank"}. I am skilled in interdisciplinary research & project management in both academic and industry settings.

Research Expertise: 
----
* Next Generation Sequencing data analysis
* Single Cell RNA-sequencing analysis with Hashtags, Antibody Capture and Immune Repertoire
* Metagenomics analysis (viral and bacterial)
* Immunology and human genetics 
* Yeast and bacterial microbiology
